“
Oyu Tolgoi” LLC
(
the ‘
Company’
) would like to invite potential and/or experienced suppliers to submit their
Expression of Interest (EOI)
in relation to
Supply of EPC Service for 60-ton Gantry Crane Installation at Marshalling Yard
.
[An EOI is only a preliminary step to a formal tender process to understand and identify whether there are potential suppliers in the market that are interested in and capable of satisfying the procurement needs]
Scope description
:
A new 60-ton gantry crane will be constructed at the Marshalling Yard of Oyu Tolgoi Site. The Company has procured two gantry cranes from China, and assembly and fabrication process is currently underway. The project will follow an EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) model, with the scope outlined below. Interested parties may bid for the full EPC package or individual Engineering or Service Material supply components.
The scope of work includes but is not limited to following:
1. Engineering Services
The scope includes all necessary engineering design work, excluding the gantry crane and its foundation. Key responsibilities include:
Electrical design and calculations for the crane power supply from the existing substation.
Design of all related supports and load calculations.
All engineering work must be performed by licensed professionals with valid government approvals.
All drawings shall comply with Oyu Tolgoi standards and specifications.
2. Procurement and Construction Services
The construction scope includes:
Supply all materials in accordance with the approved design, specifications, and requirements.
Identify and manage long-lead items such as crane rails, rail clips, and associated electrical components.
On-site assembly of the two gantry cranes.
Installation of the electrical power supply system.
Installation of crane rails, clips and alignment.
Testing and commissioning of the entire system.
Interested and experienced suppliers are invited to submit their EOI and must include/attach, as a minimum, the following documentations/information:
Mandatory documents:
State registration certificate (copy)*
HSE policy & procedures (copy)*
Business Code of Conduct (copy)*
Audited Financial statement from the last 3 years (copy)*
Brief introduction of company (copy)*
Ownership/ shareholder information such as Company charter (copy)*
Executive team structure/ introduction (copy)*
Organization structure & Total manpower (copy)*
* If the latest copies are already uploaded in OASIS Supplier Database, please make a note in the submission document(s) and no need to re-submit.
Scope-specific documents:
Scope-specific experience
Customer reference (at least 2)
Applicable license and permissions.
